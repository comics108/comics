Cutting mode — devices

Third mode in the top-bar switch: [Edit | Lettering | Cutting]. Same three-pane shape as Lettering — region rail on the left, review card on the right, page canvas in the middle. Functional on macOS only this iteration; on iPad and iPhone the switch is visible but disabled, with an honest explanation on tap. Source page: page-014 photo, regions from the local pipeline.
